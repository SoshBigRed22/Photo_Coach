Privacy Policy for Photo Coach

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Photo Coach ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal portfolio project designed to help users improve their photo-taking and try virtual looks (such as piercings and hairstyles).

Information We Collect

  • Photos and Images: Users may upload photos for face alignment guidance and virtual try-on features. These images are processed temporarily on our servers and are not stored permanently unless you explicitly choose to save them.
  • Pinterest Data: If you connect your Pinterest account, we receive basic profile information (such as username and profile picture) solely to enable the virtual try-on feature. We do not post anything to your Pinterest account.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical information (such as browser type and anonymized usage statistics) may be collected to improve the application.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide face tracking, alignment guidance, and photo quality ratings.
  • To overlay virtual items (piercings, hairstyles, etc.) on your uploaded photos.
  • To improve the application through bug fixes and feature development.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your uploaded photos and Pinterest information are used only within the app and are not shared with third parties except:

  • As required by law.
  • With service providers (such as Render) who help host and run the application.

Data Retention

Uploaded photos are processed temporarily and automatically deleted after processing unless you choose to keep them. Pinterest connection data can be revoked by disconnecting your account.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Request deletion of your data by contacting the developer.
  • Revoke Pinterest access at any time through Pinterest settings.

Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via GitHub or the contact form on the site.

This privacy policy is provided for a personal portfolio project and may be updated in the future.
