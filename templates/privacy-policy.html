Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Overview

Photo Coach is a web application for camera-based image analysis and style exploration. This page explains what information may be processed when you use the site.

Information You Provide

  • Photos you capture or upload for analysis.
  • Optional Pinterest links or Pinterest account content when you choose to connect/import.

How Images Are Processed

Uploaded or captured images are sent to the backend only to perform requested features such as analysis, tracking support, or overlay processing.

The app is designed for transient processing and does not intentionally maintain a permanent image gallery of your uploads.

Pinterest Integration

If you connect Pinterest, the app uses the granted scopes to fetch profile, board, and pin metadata needed for in-app selection features.

Pinterest session/token data may be stored temporarily in server memory for active sessions.

Cookies and Local Storage

The site may use browser local storage for convenience features, including saved inspiration entries and connection handles to improve user experience between visits on the same device.

Third-Party Services

This project may be hosted on services such as GitHub Pages and Render. Their infrastructure and logs may process technical request data according to their own policies.

Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect data in transit and limit unnecessary retention, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Your Choices

  • You can avoid uploading sensitive photos.
  • You can disconnect Pinterest at any time from the app UI.
  • You can clear browser storage from your browser settings.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the project owner through the public project channel or repository contact method.

This policy is provided for project transparency and may be updated as features evolve.
